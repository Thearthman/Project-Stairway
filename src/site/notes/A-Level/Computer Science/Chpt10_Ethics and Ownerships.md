# 10.02 Code of Conduct
>[! definition] Code of conduct
>The rules for content creation

## Principals for code of conduct 
1. Public - software engineer **shall act** consistenly with the public interest
2. Profession - software engineering manager shall **advance the integrity and reputation of the profession** consistent with the public interest.
3. Employer & Client - software engineers shall act in a manner that is in the **best interest of their client and employer** consistent with the public interest. 
4. Judgement - software engineer shall **maintain integrity and independence** in their professional judgement 
5. Product - software engineer shall **ensure that their products and related modifications meet the highest professional standards** possible.
6. Management - software engineering managers and leaders shall subcribe to and promote and ethical approach to the **management of software development and maintenance.**
7. Colleagues - software engineers shall be **fair to and supportive of their colleagues**. 
8. Self - lifelong learning; promote ethical approach to practice of the profession.

# Types of software licenses
- Commercial
	- needs payments for usage
	- can't resell
	- can't aceess, modify, redistribute.
- Freeware
	- Can't resell, You can use but can't modify.
	- may be limited of usage and requires payment for additional usage
- Shareware
	- full or limited usage for a period of *time* then needs payments
- Opensource
	- Can access, modify, redistribute the code.
	-  but can't sell it, or needs permission to sell it.
